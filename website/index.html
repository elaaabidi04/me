The problem with most journaling apps

They want you checking in constantly. We don't.

Most apps chase engagement — more prompts, more streaks. Overthinking isn't the goal.

Typical journaling apps

  • Push daily "insights" to keep you opening the app
  • Analyze every entry the moment you write it
  • Quietly position the AI as a stand-in therapist
  • Reward constant self-monitoring with streaks and badges

me

  • Reflects back on a calm, weekly rhythm — never daily
  • Aggregates patterns in code, not a chatbot guessing in the moment
  • Stays a companion that notices — never diagnoses, never pathologizes
  • Knows when to hold back instead of pushing you to dig deeper
